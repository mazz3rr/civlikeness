## 1. The Constitution (v3.9, unchanged)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite, world-scale map into a self-authored network of productive nodes whose cultivation is the game's center of gravity — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long, discretely-paced timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The eight pillars:** P1 finite/hidden/world-scale map · P2 generative expansion · P3 interior economy · P4 epochal ladder · P5 (H) peer competitors · P6a collective subject · P6b (H) total polity, plural endings · P7 temporal form. (All clause text, severity ladders, ladder-shape taxonomy, and leak price lists per v3.4–v3.9 amendments, standing.)

## 1a. Chassis/House — Formal Specification (v4.1, standing)

- **Chassis (P1+P2+P3+P4+P6a+P7, /30) = the definition's operational clauses** — what the player does, with what, in what, as what, in what kind of time. *How the game runs.*
- **House (P5+P6b, /10) = the relational clauses** — with whom (peers) and for what (plural declared endings + institutional interiority). *Who the play is with, and what it is for.*
- Load-bearing test: full chassis + hollow house = functioning strategy game, not Civ-like (SC4). House without chassis degenerate by definition. Not a quality judgment; a change of basis (Ch+H = Total, asserted per row).
- P6a = who drives (form — chassis); P6b = what the collective contains and where the journey ends (institutions and destiny — house).
- RV organ-map: demoted mnemonic (frame P1, drivetrain P2, engine P3, gearbox P4, neighborhood P5, driver's seat P6a, civic institutions P6b, transmission P7).

## 1b. Genealogy (v4.2 — CORRECTED; supersedes v4.1 findings item 1's thesis)

**Ancestral duality (pre-1991):** the **solitaire builder** (chassis-rich, house-zero: SimCity) and the **wargame** (chassis-poor, **house-seeded**: the peer, the military polity-domain, the conquest teleology — a minimal house in our own terms). Wargame axiom (refined): *the wargame requires an opposing agent; script-agents do not confer peer-hood* — the solitaire-wargame niche (Ambush!) faces scripts and converges on the tactics-game (XCOM, P5 0.5). Hence: no true solitary wargames at peer-grade.

**Civ's act (1991):** the builder's garden projected onto the wargame's polity skeleton, plus the arc — historically literal (SimCity + Empire/Risk + the Civilization boardgame). Plural endings' parentage: conquest from the wargame; the mega-project/spaceship from the *builder* (completion-victory is the garden's teleology).

**Two fates of the synthesis:** (1) **house-elaboration** — the Civ lineage drifting toward society-simulation (culture Civ3, religion Civ4, the completed house); (2) the **Exterminate-weighted branch** — MOO's tactical battles, MoM following, the wing Emrich named "4X" *at*. Founding trio below every mainline Civ (35.5/36.0/34.5) — now genealogically explained.

**Paradox grand strategy is downstream, not ancestral:** no house-rich tradition existed before Civ to hybridize with; EU-lineage = post-Civ offshoot taking the elaborated-house pole and shedding the garden. (Supersedes "4X = hybrid of chassis-first and house-first traditions" — the wargame was never chassis-first, and the house-forward tradition is a descendant, not a trunk.)

**"Civ-like" vs "4X" (the round-1 question, resolved):** two poles of one configuration. All Civ-likes perform the four X's (subset relation); 4X licenses heavy Exterminate-weighting; the wargame wing (Dominions: both axes hurt by one slant, 30.5, 4X-without-Civ-likeness) is 4X-but-not-Civ-like. **The community's "4X" label decomposes as verb-completeness × long arc:** EU4 fails eXplore (P1 3.5) → grand strategy; OTC lacks eXterminate (war-share absent) → economic RTS; Anno peripheral-war + compressed → builder; Dominions/Stellaris pass both gates → 4X.

**RTSes = selectively compressed 4X configurations (the compression filter):** temporal compression does not shrink the configuration uniformly — it preserves the **fast organs** (resource streams, tier races, map contest, unit control) and atrophies the **slow organs** (gardens, epochal rewrites, polity, plural endings). Hence RTS chassis 19–20 with collapsed houses. The triple correspondence (period rhyme, strongest at franchise peaks): Civ↔AoE (epochs, wonders), MOO↔StarCraft (space, asym species, tier races, tactical focus), MoM↔Warcraft (fantasy, magic, heroes/items/creeps — WC3 the peak match). "Mini-4X" holds as structure, not as label — the vernacular "4X" encodes the long arc, which P7 formalizes.
