## 1. The Constitution (v3.9 core + amendments)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite, world-scale map into a self-authored network of productive nodes whose cultivation is the game's center of gravity — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long, discretely-paced timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The eight pillars:** P1 finite/hidden/world-scale map · P2 generative expansion · P3 interior economy · P4 epochal ladder · P5 (H) peer competitors · P6a collective subject · P6b (H) total polity, plural endings · P7 temporal form. (Clause text, severity ladders, ladder-shape taxonomy, leak price lists per v3.4–v3.9, standing.)

**P4 ladder-shape taxonomy:** frozen (Civ5) · commitment (BE) · shallow-swappable (EL, **GalCiv4 policies**) · frozen-with-exits (OW) · bestowed (Dominions, AoW3) · transformation (AoW4) · swappable-with-friction (**GalCiv3 governments, 26-turn lock**) · swappable (full strength).

**P2 note:** *founding wall* documented (GalCiv3 Retribution — deliberate colony-rush suppression; the Civ5-health shape applied to founding; 4.5 tier). Present-but-suppressed founding (AoW:SM) and pure acquisition (3.0 tier) remain distinct grades.
