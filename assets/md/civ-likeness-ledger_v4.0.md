## 1. The Constitution (v3.9, unchanged)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite, world-scale map into a self-authored network of productive nodes whose cultivation is the game's center of gravity — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long, discretely-paced timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The eight pillars:**

- **P1 — Finite, hidden, world-scale map (the commons).** Clauses: initially unknown; finite; contested commons; diegetic spatial scale. CtP notes: planet standard + undersea/orbital frontiers (late-game frontier reopening, technology-gated — the Civ7 ocean-gate lineage).
- **P2 — Generative expansion.** Planting, not seizing; authored network; developed nodes; minimal-foothold arc. CtP note: Public Works (tile improvement as budget, not units) = interface variant, priced as texture — the verb survives, the instrument changes. Acquisition tier: five members, all 3.0.
- **P3 — Interior economy.** Center of gravity; self-improving nodes; evolving wide/deep dial. CtP1: ICS-family at family rate. CtP2: same, with the army-management tilt noted (gravity texture).
- **P4 — Epochal ladder.** (A) time as competition; (B) rewrites; (C) society re-encoding. CtP signature: extended future epochs (4000 BC → 3000/2300 AD), 100+ techs, swappable governments including future types — full 5.0 on both entries. Ladder-shape taxonomy: frozen · commitment · shallow-swappable · frozen-with-exits · bestowed · swappable.
- **P5 (H) — Peer competitors.** Symmetric verbs; trajectories; diplomacy architecture; AI at equal-rules reference; no re-charging absences priced elsewhere. CtP1 4.0 (weak AI, basic diplomacy; unconventional-unit rivalry layer and revolt-born civilizations as texture). CtP2 4.5 (compound-proposal diplomacy — counteroffers, threats, regard; border-aware AI; release AI still weak — architecture carries).
- **P6a — The collective subject.** Leak-detector. Severity ladder on file (5.0 pure → 0 none). CtP pair: immortal leader interface, 5.0.
- **P6b (H) — Total polity, plural endings.** P6b = (polity + endings)/2. CtP1 (4.0+3.0): law/religion/ecology as unit-verbs, not subsystems; conquest + 3000 AD clock (project victories unverified — conservatively excluded). CtP2 (4.0+3.5): conquest, World Peace (alliance with every nation), Gaia Controller science — manual-verified — plus the 2300 clock.
- **P7 — Temporal form.** P7 = (discreteness + diegetic timescale)/2. CtP pair 5.0 (turns; 7000/6300-year epochal arcs).

**Loop-spoke audit (derived field):** expansion↔economy · economy→ladder · ladder→capability · capability↔expansion. Worked examples on file.

**Chassis/house:** chassis = P1+P2+P3+P4+P6a+P7 (/30); house = P5+P6b (/10).
