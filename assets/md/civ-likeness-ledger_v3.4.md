## 1. The Constitution (v3.4)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite, world-scale map into a self-authored network of productive nodes whose cultivation is the game's center of gravity — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long, discretely-paced timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The eight pillars:**

- **P1 — Finite, hidden, world-scale map (the commons).** Clauses: initially unknown; finite; contested commons; diegetic spatial scale (deficits price, surpluses don't bonus). Values: 5.0 (×14, now incl. SMAC, BE — BE's orbital layer noted as spatial innovation) · 4.5 (MOO pair; Anno; EVE) · 3.5 (EU4/CK3; SC2) · 3.0 (XCOM 2) · 1.5 (SC4) · 1.0 (Doom) · 0.5 (ItB, Sims 4) · 0 (FM26).
- **P2 — Generative expansion.** Planting, not seizing; authored network; developed nodes; minimal-foothold arc (one-node/inherited starts price here). Values: 5.0 (×15, now incl. SMAC, BE — BE + aquatic cities) · 4.0 (SC2, SC4) · 3.5 (EVE) · 3.0 (EU4, CK3, Sims 4) · 2.5 (XCOM 2) · 1.5 (FM26) · 0 (Doom, ItB).
- **P3 — Interior economy.** Center of gravity = developing what one owns; solitaire optimizer; evolving wide/deep dial. Degeneracy shapes: ICS (Civ1/2, **SMAC — same engine, same disease, + crawler meta**), corruption-wall (Civ3), global wall (Civ5, **BE — Health is Happiness in a lab coat; RT softens texture, not shape**), age-reset (Civ7), concentration tilt (Col), negotiator-inversion (EU4), character-layer inversion (CK3), war-fuel (SC2). Max club (×7): MOO2, Civ4, Civ6, HK, Stellaris, SC4, Anno.
- **P4 — Epochal ladder.** (A) time as competition; (B) vocabulary rewrites; (C) society re-encoding — climbable AND swappable. **Society-clause taxonomy (v3.4): *frozen* ladders (Civ5 policies — accumulate, never obsolete) · *commitment* ladders (BE affinities — accumulate and specialize; transformative but irreversible) · *swappable* ladders (Civ governments, SMAC Social Engineering, Civ4 civics, Civ6 cards — re-encode at will). Only swappable satisfies clause (C) at full strength; frozen/commitment are the modern 4.5s.** Branch density/selectability inadmissible (MOO2 ruling). Values: 5.0 (×7, incl. SMAC — SE matrix is proto-civics) · 4.5 (×4, incl. BE) · 3.5 (×4) · 3.0 (×5) · 2.5 (×3) · 0.5 (Doom).
- **P5 — Peer competitors.** Symmetric access to the game's own verbs; trajectories; diplomacy architecture; AI at equal-rules reference; no re-charging absences priced elsewhere (double-jeopardy ruling). Values: 5.0 (MOO pair, Civ4, EU4, EVE) · 4.5 (×10, incl. SMAC — asymmetric agenda factions + Planetary Council, Civ2-grade AI) · 4.0 (Civ1, Col, Anno, **BE — RT diplomacy carried down by weak AI**) · 3.0 (SC2) · 0.5 (XCOM 2) · 0 (×4).
- **P6a — The collective subject.** Leak-detector. Severity ladder: 5.0 pure collective (incl. SMAC, BE — leaders are immortal interfaces, no mechanics) · 4.5 single person-leak · 4.0 substitutive relay + leveling leader · 3.5 person-within-collective · 3.0 person-primary + corporate credit · 2.0 persons-inverted · 1.0 micro-collective · 0 none.
- **P6b — Total polity, plural endings.** P6b = (polity + endings)/2. New halves: SMAC (4.0+3.5→4.0 — Civ1/2 domain profile; three non-equivalent emergent teleologies incl. Transcendence, the genre's most literal apotheosis) · BE (4.0+3.5→4.0 — quest system ruled TEXTURE not teleology; five victories = three real routes, gate-family fiction-variants discounted). Column continuous 0.5→5.0.
- **P7 — Temporal form.** P7 = (discreteness + diegetic timescale)/2. Compression-collapse law. SMAC 5.0 (turns + centuries, colonization→transcendence). BE 5.0 (turns + epochal transformation arc). Values unchanged otherwise.

## 3. Protocols (all standing)

1. Final form declared before scoring (verified or trivially stable frozen public record — SC2/Doom/ItB/XCOM/SMAC/BE precedent).
2. Pre-registration — component-derived only; non-lineage registrations carry the measured-bias caveat.
3. Anchor discipline — pull column min/max before scoring each pillar.
4. Min/max recap — every round.
5. No double jeopardy — one design decision, one clause (distinct clauses, distinct damage excepted).
6. Symmetric precedents — no rule engineered to move one game's score; selectability inadmissible.
7. Factual negatives verified before assertion.
8. Shipped mechanics only — announced content = audit trigger.
9. No mind-reading.
10. Optional modes are house rules; default configuration is the game.
11. Outliers-first.
12. Derivation arithmetic must sum; booking must follow the stated formula.
13. Post-verification component deltas disclosed as deltas.
14. Table integrity — row-count reconciled every round-end; no row dropped silently.
