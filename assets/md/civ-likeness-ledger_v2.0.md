Single source of truth for the Civ-likeness exercise. Conversation memory is not authoritative; this file is. Re-read before any scoring round. Updated at the end of every round.

---

## 1. The Constitution (v2.0)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite map into a self-authored network of productive nodes — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The seven pillars:**

- **P1 — Finite, hidden map (the commons).** Initially unknown (information as resource); finite (the frontier closes); a *contested commons* — closure by encroachment, not enumeration; chokepoints, adjacency, crowding. (MOO pair at 4.5: node graph, closure by enumeration — reason on file.)
- **P2 — Generative expansion.** Planting seeds (founding nodes), not just seizing pre-made; the network is authored; nodes are developed (terrain, population, infrastructure).
- **P3 — Interior economy.** The center of gravity of play: terrain improvement, population growth, infrastructure queues, yield tuning. The wide/deep tension must be an *evolving dial* — documented degeneracy shapes: ICS collapse (Civ1/2), punishment-wall (Civ3 corruption), global wall (Civ5 happiness), age-reset depreciation (Civ7), late-game concentration tilt (Col).
- **P4 — Epochal ladder.** Three components: (A) time as an axis of competition; (B) vocabulary rewrites — units, actions, "what strong looks like"; (C) society re-encoding — the ladder must be climbable AND the society layer swappable. **Branch density is inadmissible** (ruling that struck MOO2 from 4.0 to 3.5).
- **P5 — Peer competitors.** Symmetric access to the verbs; own rise/stagnation trajectories; diplomacy texture; AI competence measured at reference difficulty (equal-rules level: Civ4 Noble; CoM Normal).
- **P6a — The collective subject.** The player is a persistent collective; units, cities, leaders are instruments. Leak thresholds: named, persistent, *leveling* individuals holding agency (MOO2 heroes −0.5; Civ6 governors −0.5); subject substitution (HK cumulative relay −0.5; Civ7 substitutive relay + leveling named leader −1.0); person-as-subject (MoM −1.5: wizard is the subject, empire is property, plus hero layer).
- **P6b — Total polity, plural endings.** Two components, each ~2.5 pts: **(polity half)** research/culture/economy/faith/diplomacy/military modeled as subsystems; **(endings half)** plural, non-equivalent, *emergent* teleologies. Discounts: authored/boss teleologies (Antaran precedent), monism (HK: many routes, one currency, one ending), deferral (Civ7: two-thirds of the arc has no victory condition).

## 2. The Scale (v2.0)

- Per pillar: **0 to 5.0, steps of 0.5** (11 grades). Totals /35.
- **0 is an absolute zero: pillar absent.** 5 = canonical full realization.
- Anchor ladder: **5** canonical · **4.5** realized, one named flaw · **4** realized, multiple flaws · **3.5** substantially realized, one clause absent · **3** partial · **2.5** partial-marginal · **2** vestigial-to-partial, present but not load-bearing · **1** vestigial · **0.5** trace element, structurally inert · **0** absent.
- Component arithmetic may compute in quarters; **booked scores are halves**. Booking convention: round half up, stated on the record (HK 3.75→4.0; Col 3.25→3.5 precedents).
- Retroactively inert: the 0-floor changed no pre-existing score (nothing sat below 0.5).

## 3. Protocols (all standing)

1. **Final form declared before scoring** — fully-DLC-realized / canonical complete form (Civ3=Conquests, Civ4=BtS, Civ5=BNW, Civ6=GS, Civ7=current patch level, MoM=Caster of Magic, Col=1994 original, HK=Together We Rule).
2. **Pre-registration** — predictions lodged in this file before scoring; opened only after pillar scores are written.
3. **Anchor discipline** — before scoring a pillar, pull the column min/max and place the candidate against them.
4. **Min/max recap** — every round ends with a one-line justification for each column's current min and max.
5. **No double jeopardy** — one design decision, one clause (1UPT charged at P5 only; Civ7 age-reset exception: distinct clauses, distinct damage, both charged).
6. **Symmetric precedents** — a ruling binds all past and future scores (branch-density ruling struck MOO2 retroactively).
7. **Factual negatives must be verified before assertion** (the Humankind/Together-We-Rule lesson: the "cosmetics only" parenthetical was wrong about one expansion that added a world congress, espionage, embassies).
8. **Shipped mechanics only** — announced content = audit trigger (Civ7: *Earthrise* expansion and *Atomic Age* free update will trigger full re-audit of P3/P4 reset deductions and deferred-victory arithmetic).
9. **No mind-reading** — neither assumptions about a game's deserved score nor models of the user's expectations may steer scoring. Mechanics + anchors only. (Adopted after round 9; violation had appeared in private reasoning.)
10. **Optional modes are house rules; the default configuration is the game** (Civ6 New Frontier ruling: Heroes & Legends toggles don't count).
11. **Outliers-first** — when a column grows or a prompt alleges drift, audit the column's extremes first, prompted or not (this caught the MOO2 P4 strike-down).

## 5. The Boundary (v2.0 — adopted this round)

**The scored domain: PC strategy games in which the player primarily develops a persistent collective entity, in competition or coexistence with rival agents, within a shared spatial game-world, across an arc of play.**

Four admission clauses:
1. **Strategy is the primary mode** (where the session's decision-density lives).
2. **The persistent collective is the unit of agency.**
3. **A shared spatial world** (territory acted within and over; contested or not).
4. **An arc of play** (discrete start; end-state or clock).

Design philosophy: **wide gate, discriminating scale.** The gate admits everything for which "how Civ-like?" is a real question; the zero-anchored scale does the discriminating.

**Ruled IN (pending):** EU4, Stellaris, CK3, StarCraft II (melee), SimCity 4, Anno 1800, Old World, Endless Legend, AoE2, Dominions 5, Total War (campaign layer), Offworld Trading Company, Alpha Centauri, Beyond Earth (the last two are inside-boundary backfill, not boundary tests).

**Ruled OUT (with reasons):**
- *XCOM, Into the Breach* — tactics-primary (battles are the object; strategy layer selects battles) AND no commons (no territory held).
- *EVE Online* — no arc (persistent service); the no-arc extreme of the same clause that discounts EU4's endings.
- *Football Manager* — no spatial world (league table, not territory).
- *The Sims* — individual subject.
- *Doom* — fails all four clauses (the trivial control case).

**Precedent note:** tactical sub-games do not exclude (MoM, MOO2 scored; Total War eligible) — battles as a *sub-game* are fine; battles as *the point* exclude.
