## 1. The Constitution (v2.0)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite map into a self-authored network of productive nodes — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The seven pillars:**

- **P1 — Finite, hidden map (the commons).** Initially unknown (information as resource); finite (the frontier closes); a *contested commons* — closure by encroachment, not enumeration; chokepoints, adjacency, crowding. MOO pair 4.5: node graph, closure by enumeration. EU4 3.5: commons canonical, discovery absent (atlas map). **Hypothesis (Round D surgery candidate): P1 is two components — commons and discovery — which can diverge independently (MOO: discovery-strong/commons-weak; EU4: the mirror).**
- **P2 — Generative expansion.** Planting seeds (founding nodes), not just seizing pre-made; the network is authored; nodes are developed. EU4 3.0: first break — annexation-dominant, colonization the only generative verb, inheritance not authorship.
- **P3 — Interior economy.** The center of gravity of play: terrain improvement, population growth, infrastructure queues, yield tuning; the game is substantially a solitaire optimizer with rivals orbiting. Wide/deep must be an evolving dial. Degeneracy shapes on file: ICS (Civ1/2), corruption-wall (Civ3), global wall (Civ5), age-reset depreciation (Civ7), concentration tilt (Col), **negotiator-inversion (EU4: economy as instrumentation, population abstracted away — 3.0, column floor)**.
- **P4 — Epochal ladder.** Components: (A) time as axis of competition; (B) vocabulary rewrites; (C) society re-encoding — climbable AND swappable. Branch density inadmissible (MOO2 strike-down). **Continuity trending: EU4's 3.0 is the first intermediate value (component-derived) between Col 2.5 and the 3.5 stratum.**
- **P5 — Peer competitors.** Symmetric verbs, own trajectories, diplomacy architecture, AI competence at equal-rules reference difficulty. EU4 5.0: fourth max, first earned primarily on architecture (deepest diplomacy in ledger).
- **P6a — The collective subject.** Persistent collective; individuals are instruments. Leak clauses: leveling named individuals (MOO2 heroes, Civ6 governors, Stellaris leaders — each −0.5); subject substitution (HK cumulative relay −0.5; Civ7 substitutive + leveling leader −1.0); person-as-subject (MoM −1.0, hero layer −0.5).
- **P6b — Total polity, plural endings.** Polity half: research/culture/economy/faith/diplomacy/military as subsystems. Endings half: plural, non-equivalent, emergent teleologies. **Endings-half strata forming: 5 (plural emergent) / 4.5 / 3 (two clean: MOO1) / 2.5 (route-monism: HK; score+conquest+authored-crisis: Stellaris) / 2.0 (clock only: EU4).** Paradox house signature: polity-maximal (both 5.0), endings-poor (2.0–2.5).

## 2. The Scale (v2.0)

- Per pillar: **0 to 5.0, steps of 0.5** (11 grades). Totals /35. **0 = pillar absent; 5 = canonical realization.**
- Anchor ladder: **5** canonical · **4.5** realized, one named flaw · **4** realized, multiple flaws · **3.5** substantially realized, one clause absent · **3** partial · **2.5** partial-marginal · **2** vestigial-to-partial, present but not load-bearing · **1** vestigial · **0.5** trace · **0** absent.
- Component arithmetic may compute in quarters; booked scores are halves. Booking: round half up, stated on record (HK 3.75→4.0; Col 3.25→3.5; Stellaris 3.75→4.0).

## 3. Protocols (all standing)

1. **Final form declared before scoring** — fully-DLC-realized / canonical complete form.
2. **Pre-registration** — predictions lodged before scoring; opened only after pillar scores are written.
3. **Anchor discipline** — pull column min/max before scoring each pillar.
4. **Min/max recap** — every round ends with a one-line justification for each column's current min and max.
5. **No double jeopardy** — one design decision, one clause (exception: distinct clauses taking distinct damage, e.g. Civ7 age-reset at P3 and P4).
6. **Symmetric precedents** — rulings bind all past and future scores.
7. **Factual negatives must be verified before assertion** (HK/TWR lesson; reaffirmed Round A: Stellaris post-BioGenesis content verified before final-form declaration — Nomads expansion and base consolidation found, Willpower registered as trigger).
8. **Shipped mechanics only** — announced content = audit trigger.
9. **No mind-reading** — no assumptions about games' deserved scores or the user's expectations; mechanics + anchors only.
10. **Optional modes are house rules; the default configuration is the game** (Civ6 New Frontier; Stellaris Q4 2026 Scenario Packs).
11. **Outliers-first** — audit column extremes when a column grows or drift is alleged.

## 5. The Boundary (v2.0)

**The scored domain: PC strategy games in which the player primarily develops a persistent collective entity, in competition or coexistence with rival agents, within a shared spatial game-world, across an arc of play.** Four admission clauses: strategy-primary; collective unit of agency; shared spatial world; arc of play. **Wide gate, discriminating scale.**

**Ruled IN (pending):** CK3, StarCraft II (melee), SimCity 4, Anno 1800, Old World, Endless Legend, AoE2, Dominions 5, Total War (campaign layer), Offworld Trading Company, Alpha Centauri, Beyond Earth (backfill).
**Ruled OUT:** XCOM, Into the Breach (tactics-primary, no commons) · EVE Online (no arc) · Football Manager (no spatial world) · The Sims (individual subject) · Doom (trivial).
