## 1. The Constitution (v3.3)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite, world-scale map into a self-authored network of productive nodes **whose cultivation is the game's center of gravity** — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long, discretely-paced timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The eight pillars:**

- **P1 — Finite, hidden, world-scale map (the commons).** Clauses: (1) initially unknown; (2) finite; (3) contested commons; (4) diegetic spatial scale (world+; deficits price, surpluses don't bonus). Values: 5.0 (×12) · 4.5 (MOO pair; Anno; EVE) · 3.5 (EU4/CK3 atlas; SC2) · 3.0 (XCOM 2) · 1.5 (SC4) · 1.0 (Doom) · 0.5 (ItB, Sims 4) · 0 (FM26 — the league table is not territory).
- **P2 — Generative expansion.** Planting, not seizing; authored network; developed nodes. **The arc runs from minimal foothold to network — one-node or inherited starts price here (XCOM 2.5, FM26 1.5).** Values: 5.0 (×13) · 4.0 (SC2, SC4) · 3.5 (EVE) · 3.0 (EU4, CK3, Sims 4) · 2.5 (XCOM 2) · 1.5 (FM26) · 0 (Doom, ItB).
- **P3 — Interior economy.** Center of gravity = developing what one owns; solitaire optimizer with rivals orbiting; evolving wide/deep dial. (Now also in the definition sentence.) Values: 5.0 (×7: MOO2, Civ4, Civ6, HK, Stellaris, SC4, Anno) · 4.0 (EVE) · 3.5 (Sims 4) · 3.0 (EU4, FM26) · 2.5 (CK3, SC2, XCOM 2) · 0.5 (Doom).
- **P4 — Epochal ladder.** (A) time as competition; (B) vocabulary rewrites; (C) society re-encoding (climbable AND swappable). **Branch density / selectability inadmissible (MOO2 ruling — reaffirmed against external Fix 2).** Values: 5.0 (×6) · 4.5 (×3) · 3.5 (MOO pair, MoM, Anno) · 3.0 (EU4, CK3, SC2, XCOM 2, FM26) · 2.5 (Col, Sims 4, EVE) · 0.5 (Doom).
- **P5 — Peer competitors.** Symmetric access to **the game's own verbs**; own trajectories; diplomacy architecture; AI fidelity priced at the equal-rules reference against column precedent (Civ1 freebie-AI and Col bonus-peer precedents price subsidy at 4.0-grade). **Ruling (external review): absences priced in other pillars (no research → P4; no victory paths → P6b-endings) may not be re-charged here — double jeopardy.** Values: 5.0 (MOO pair, Civ4, EU4, EVE) · 4.5 (×8, incl. FM26) · 4.0 (Civ1, Col, **Anno — held after adjudication; minority 3.5 on record, not booked**) · 3.0 (SC2) · 0.5 (XCOM 2) · 0 (SC4, Doom, ItB, Sims 4).
- **P6a — The collective subject.** Leak-detector. **Empirical severity ladder (observed prices):** 5.0 pure collective · 4.5 single person-leak (MOO2, Civ6, Stellaris, HK) · 4.0 substitutive relay + leveling leader (Civ7) · 3.5 person-within-collective (EVE) or person-as-subject single-charge (MoM) · 3.0 person-primary agency with corporate-persistence credit (CK3) · 2.0 persons-inverted: instruments become subjects (Sims 4, XCOM 2, FM26) · 1.0 micro-collective (ItB) · 0 none (Doom). Max club 11/24.
- **P6b — Total polity, plural endings.** P6b = (polity + endings)/2. Continuous 0.5→5.0; halves on file per game (unchanged from v3.2).
- **P7 — Temporal form.** P7 = (discreteness + diegetic timescale)/2. Compression-collapse law. Values: 5.0 (×12) · 4.0 (EU4, CK3, Stellaris, XCOM 2) · 3.5 (FM26) · 3.0 (Anno, ItB, Sims 4) · 2.0 (SC4, EVE) · 1.0 (SC2) · 0.5 (Doom).

**Derived diagnostic (no score): the loop-spoke audit.** Four couplings, documented per game: **expansion↔economy · economy→ladder · ladder→capability · capability↔expansion** — do they circulate on one strategic layer? Worked examples: *Civ* — all four, compounding (the snowball). *Anno 1800* — four spokes present and tight (islands→chains→tiers→fleets→islands), MINUS the raced-knowledge spoke (P4-priced) and deep-diplomacy spoke (P5-priced); the mimcry is real: what Anno lacks is duration and destiny, not circulation. *SC2* — complete loop at match scale (integration is not its deficit; compression is — P7-priced). *EU4* — loop tight, authorship absent (P2-priced). *SC4* — economy→economy only; no expansion/military spokes. *EVE* — loop real and player-driven, knowledge spoke absent. Remaining games: fill opportunistically.

**Chassis/house:** chassis = P1+P2+P3+P4+P6a+P7 (/30); house = P5+P6b (/10). Notable adjacencies: Anno (25.5/6.5) and EU4 (21.5/8.5) — the mirror partial-Civs (garden-without-time vs time-without-garden).

## 3. Protocols (all standing)

1. Final form declared before scoring (verified, not remembered).
2. Pre-registration — component-derived only. Registrations on non-lineage games carry the measured-bias caveat (8/9 lifetime misses same sign).
3. Anchor discipline — pull column min/max before scoring each pillar.
4. Min/max recap — every round.
5. No double jeopardy — one design decision, one clause (exception: distinct clauses, distinct damage). **Reaffirmed against external Fix 1: an absence priced in one pillar may not be re-charged as an AI deficiency in another.**
6. Symmetric precedents — rulings bind all past and future scores. **Reaffirmed against external Fix 2: no rule may be engineered to move one game's score; selectability remains inadmissible (MOO2 ruling).**
7. Factual negatives verified before assertion.
8. Shipped mechanics only — announced content = audit trigger.
9. No mind-reading.
10. Optional modes are house rules; default configuration is the game.
11. Outliers-first.
12. Derivation arithmetic must sum; booking must follow the stated formula.
13. Post-verification component deltas disclosed as deltas.
14. **Table integrity: row-count reconciled against the previous version at every round-end; no row dropped silently (SC4 incident — error class #3, data loss, externally caught).**
