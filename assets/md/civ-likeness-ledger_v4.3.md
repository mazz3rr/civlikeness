## 1. The Constitution (v3.9, unchanged)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite, world-scale map into a self-authored network of productive nodes whose cultivation is the game's center of gravity — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long, discretely-paced timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The eight pillars:** P1 finite/hidden/world-scale map · P2 generative expansion · P3 interior economy · P4 epochal ladder · P5 (H) peer competitors · P6a collective subject · P6b (H) total polity, plural endings · P7 temporal form. (All clause text, severity ladders, ladder-shape taxonomy, and leak price lists per v3.4–v3.9 amendments, standing.)

## 1b. Genealogy (v4.2, standing + v4.3 confirmations)

**Ancestral duality:** solitaire builder (chassis-rich, house-zero) × wargame (chassis-poor, house-seeded: peer, military domain, conquest teleology). Wargame axiom: requires an opposing agent; script-agents don't confer peer-hood.

**Civ's act (1991):** garden projected onto the wargame's polity skeleton + the arc. Plural endings' parentage: conquest from the wargame; mega-project completion from the builder.

**Two fates:** house-elaboration (Civ lineage) and the Exterminate-weighted branch (MOO → MoM; "4X" named at MOO). Paradox = post-Civ offshoot (elaborated house, shed garden).

**"Civ-like" vs "4X":** two poles of one configuration; community 4X label = verb-completeness × long arc.

**RTSes = selectively compressed 4X configurations** (fast organs preserved, slow organs atrophied). Triple rhyme: Civ↔AoE, MOO↔SC, MoM↔WC.

**v4.3 confirmations (fantasy wing, quantified):**
- **Specialization gradient: MoM 34.5 → HoMM3 26.0 → Warlords III 24.0.** The late-90s descendants of the MoM wing shed chassis organs (founding, research, sandbox epic) and hypertrophied the hero layer — the wing specialized toward the wargame pole. AoW3's registered band (30–33) sits centered in the gap.
- **The scenario-arc is the wargame pole's native structure** — bounded, authored engagements with mapmaker-set victory conditions (hits P6b endings, P7 timescale), vs the Civ pole's emergent sandbox epic. Named axis difference.
- **Genealogical fossils:** HoMM3's Grail victory = the builder's completion-teleology preserved inside a wargame.
- **Configuration ties:** HoMM3 = AoE2 exactly (26.0; 20.5/5.5 vs 20.0/6.0 — MoM's fantasy heir and the historical RTS converge from opposite axes); Warlords III = SC2 exactly (24.0 — the wargame pole by two roads).
- Market-segmentation epigraph on file (Darklords player, 2021: Civ "too much like having a job," WL3 "hits the spot" — the pole-choice felt by players, pre-rubric).
