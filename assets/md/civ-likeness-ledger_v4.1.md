## 1. The Constitution (v3.9, unchanged)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite, world-scale map into a self-authored network of productive nodes whose cultivation is the game's center of gravity — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long, discretely-paced timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The eight pillars:** P1 finite/hidden/world-scale map · P2 generative expansion · P3 interior economy · P4 epochal ladder · P5 (H) peer competitors · P6a collective subject · P6b (H) total polity, plural endings · P7 temporal form. ## 1a. Chassis/House — Formal Specification (v4.1)

**The division is the definition's own clause structure:**

- **Chassis (P1+P2+P3+P4+P6a+P7, /30) = the operational clauses** — what the player does, with what, in what, as what, in what kind of time: the map (P1), the foothold→network conversion (P2), the cultivation economy (P3), the ladder (P4), the subject's form (P6a), the time-substrate (P7). *How the game runs.*
- **House (P5+P6b, /10) = the relational clauses** — with whom (P5: peers) and for what (P6b: plural declared endings, plus the institutional interiority that makes the collective a civilization rather than a war-machine — the coequality clause). *Who the play is with, and what it is for.*

**The load-bearing test:** full chassis + hollow house = a functioning strategy game that is not Civ-like (SC4 19.5/1.0). House without chassis is degenerate by definition — peers and destiny require something to be a peer *of* and a destiny *for*. Hence the names, literally: chassis = load-bearing structure; house = the superstructure that makes it a dwelling for a civilization.

**What the division is not:** not a quality judgment (SC4 and FM26 are masterpieces at opposite corners); not new information — a change of basis over the same eight scores (Ch+H = Total, per-row asserted); not imposed categories — the family signatures below are observed clusters in this 34-game sample.

**P6a/P6b boundary:** P6a = who drives (the subject's *form* — chassis); P6b = what the collective contains and where the journey ends (institutions and destiny — house).

**RV organ-map (demoted to mnemonic, retained for continuity of the audit trail):** frame P1, drivetrain P2, engine P3, gearbox P4, neighborhood P5, driver's seat P6a, civic institutions P6b, transmission P7.

Anno's high score with "not-Civ" feel = chassis-first tradition at masterpiece level; the synthesis it skipped lives in the house.
2. **Orthogonality proofs:** equal totals from opposite corners (SC2 19.0/5.0 = CK3 19.0/8.0 in total, nothing else); EU4 vs Stellaris — identical houses (8.5 = 8.5), entire 7.0 gap in chassis ("Paradox-ness lives in the house, Civ-likeness in the chassis").
3. **Mirror pairs:** Anno/EU4 (garden-without-time vs time-without-garden) · Dominions/EU4 (23.5/7.0 vs 21.5/8.5 — most extreme divergence) · TWH3/CK3 (20.5/6.5 vs 19.0/8.0) · SC4/SC2 · OTC/SC2.
4. **Iteration budgets localize:** CtP1→CtP2 pure house (29.5→29.5 / 7.5→8.5); SMAC = Civ2 + house (Transcendence); BE = Civ5 − house; EL→HK house trade (diplomacy +0.5, endings −1.5); Civ5→Civ6 chassis rebuild, house flat; Activision iterated house while Firaxis iterated chassis (1999–2000, convergent totals).
5. **Twins:** CtP2 ≡ SMAC — identical on all eight pillars and both axes (29.5/8.5) via disjoint components. Stellaris = the only Paradox game with a strong chassis (28.5/8.5) — the house-first studio's crossover.
6. **The failed corner (EVE):** the predicted house-without-chassis extreme was the ledger's worst registration miss (+12.0); EVE's actual chassis is 20.0 (spatial-social elite, temporally hollow). The true house-heavy low-chassis specimens are Paradox's. The quadrant has an asymmetric hole, documented.
