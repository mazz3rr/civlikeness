## 1. The Constitution (v3.9)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite, world-scale map into a self-authored network of productive nodes whose cultivation is the game's center of gravity — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long, discretely-paced timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The eight pillars:**

- **P1 — Finite, hidden, world-scale map (the commons).** Clauses: initially unknown; finite; contested commons; diegetic spatial scale. Dominions 6 reason-file completed: hidden map graph from start; multiple planes (cave underworld + ritual-gated void — MoM two-plane and ritual-gated-frontier precedents); thrones as objectives.
- **P2 — Generative expansion.** Planting, not seizing; authored network; developed nodes; minimal-foothold arc. Acquisition tier, five members (EU4, CK3, TWH3, Dominions 6, commercial-adjacent Anno), all 3.0.
- **P3 — Interior economy.** Center of gravity; self-improving nodes; evolving wide/deep dial. Dominion scales = deepest society-economy coupling scored; extreme scales (4–5) noted as intensification of the bestowed shape.
- **P4 — Epochal ladder.** (A) time as competition; (B) rewrites; (C) society re-encoding. Ladder-shape taxonomy: frozen · commitment · shallow-swappable · frozen-with-exits · bestowed · swappable. Dominions notes: legendary spells = commitment-shaped apex inside the magic ladder; ritual-gated planes = ladder-rungs opening space.
- **P5 (H) — Peer competitors.** Symmetric verbs; trajectories; diplomacy architecture; AI at equal-rules reference; no re-charging absences priced elsewhere. **Dominions 6 corrected (v3.9): no longer the void case — binding NAPs with AI, war-declaration gate, status visibility; rudimentary (one treaty type, no alliances/tribute/trade), = the 3.5 tier (AoE2's stances-and-tribute grade). The total diplomacy void belongs to Dominions 1–5; Dom6 ended the 20-year tradition. 3.5-tier: AoE2, TWH3, Dominions 6.**
- **P6a — The collective subject.** Leak-detector. Severity ladder: 5.0 pure · 4.5 single leak · 4.0 substitutive relay / theocratic fusion (Dominions: person-as-subject −1.0, heroes −0.5, +0.5 god-persisting-through-dominion) · 3.5 person-within-collective / person-as-subject · 3.0 person-primary + corporate credit · 2.0 persons-inverted · 1.0 micro-collective · 0 none.
- **P6b (H) — Total polity, plural endings.** P6b = (polity + endings)/2. Dominions (4.0+3.0): religion maximal and coequal; elimination + throne ascension; Cataclysm = pressure event, uncharged per Stellaris precedent (harsher in Dom6 — texture on an uncharged system).
- **P7 — Temporal form.** P7 = (discreteness + diegetic timescale)/2. Compression-collapse law. Sub-scales: asynchronous/simultaneous turns 5 · active-pause ticks 2.5 · suspension-pause continuous 0.5. Dominions 4.5 (simultaneous turns; months-per-turn, years-long arcs).

**Loop-spoke audit (derived field):** expansion↔economy · economy→ladder · ladder→capability · capability↔expansion. Worked examples on file.

**Chassis/house:** chassis = P1+P2+P3+P4+P6a+P7 (/30); house = P5+P6b (/10). Standing column; Ch+H = Total per row. Most extreme divergence pair: Dominions 6 (23.5/7.0) vs EU4 (21.5/8.5) — near-miss totals, opposite shapes (amended v3.9: no longer an exact tie).
