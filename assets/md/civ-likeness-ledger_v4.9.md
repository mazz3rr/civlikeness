## 1. The Constitution (v3.9 core + amendments; full clause text restored v4.9 per protocol 18)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite, world-scale map into a self-authored network of productive nodes whose cultivation is the game's center of gravity — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long, discretely-paced timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The eight pillars (full clause text):**

- **P1 — Finite, hidden, world-scale map (the commons).** Clauses: initially unknown; finite; contested commons; diegetic spatial scale. Pricing: fixed authored maps −0.5 at high quality (TWH3/EU4 line); sub-Civ scale penalized within the diegetic-scale clause (spatial-compression addendum — AoW line, Gladius-class single-world maps). Three-state fog (fogged/revealed/visible) + scan phases can carry hiddenness where brief (OTC).
- **P2 — Generative expansion.** Planting, not seizing; authored network; developed nodes; minimal-foothold arc. Acquisition tier (seizing-adjacent expansion architectures, all P2 3.0): EU4 (diplomatic), CK3 (dynastic), TWH3 (military) — the genre triplet — plus Dominions 6 and commercial-adjacent Anno. Founding-wall tier 4.5 (GalCiv3 Retribution: deliberate colony-rush kill, pop growth 0.1→0.01). Founding suppression priced here (AoW2/SM wizard-tower resurrection — the designers' anti-Civ intent quantified).
- **P3 — Interior economy.** Center of gravity; self-improving nodes; evolving wide/deep dial. Dominions scales = deepest society-economy coupling scored. Resource-tier depth above the standard dial prices 4.5 (ZEPHON: 11 tiered resources, upper strata research-gated).
- **P4 — Epochal ladder.** (A) time as competition; (B) vocabulary rewrites; (C) society re-encoding. Branch density/selectability inadmissible (MOO2 ruling → protocols 4, 11). Ladder-shape taxonomy (eight): *frozen* (Civ5 policies) · *commitment* (BE affinities) · *shallow-swappable* (EL Empire Plan — bonus-allocation, not re-encoding; GalCiv4 policies) · *frozen-with-exits* (OW laws + religion) · *bestowed* (Dominions, AoW3 class) · *transformation* (AoW4 tomes — commitment-shaped society re-encoding delivered through research; the fantasy wing's first (C)-clause at strength) · *swappable-with-friction* (GalCiv3 governments, 26-turn lock) · *swappable* (Civ governments, SMAC SE, Civ4 civics, Civ6 cards — full strength).
- **P5 (H) — Peer competitors.** Symmetric access to the game's own verbs; trajectories; diplomacy architecture; AI at equal-rules reference; no re-charging absences priced elsewhere. Max club (×6): MOO pair, Civ4, EU4, EVE, OW (Soren Johnson holds two, both AI-earned), GalCiv2 (Wardell — the two designer-signature AIs). 3.5-tier = stances-and-tribute / basic-NAP grade: AoE2, TWH3, Dominions 6 (binding NAPs + war-declaration gate — Dom6 ended the 1–5 diplomacy void), Warlords III. 3.0-tier = symmetric peers, zero diplomacy, competent AI: SC2, OTC, HoMM3, WC3, CoE5, Gladius. Script-agents do not confer peer-hood (wargame axiom — XCOM 2, 0.5).
- **P6a — The collective subject.** Leak-detector. Severity ladder: 5.0 pure collective · 4.5 single person-leak (MOO2 heroes, Civ6 governors, Stellaris leaders, HK, EL heroes, OW [−0.5 leveling king, −0.5 person-fate-binds-state, +0.5 dynasty persistence], SMAC/BE leaders-as-interface, TWH3 lords, GalCiv4 leaders) · 4.0 substitutive relay + leveling leader (Civ7) / theocratic fusion (Dominions: person-as-subject −1.0, heroes −0.5, god-persisting-through-dominion +0.5) · 3.5 person-within-collective (EVE) / person-as-subject (MoM) · 3.0 person-primary + corporate credit (CK3) · 2.0 persons-inverted (Sims 4, XCOM 2, FM26) · 1.0 micro-collective (ItB) · 0 none (Doom). **Ingredient-vs-architecture law: the same person-layer prices −0.5 as organ (OW), −1.0 as identity-substitution (Civ7), −2.0 as architecture (CK3).**
- **P6b (H) — Total polity, plural endings.** P6b = (polity + endings)/2. Polity half: research/culture/economy/faith/diplomacy/military as subsystems. Endings strata: 5 (plural emergent) · 4.5 · 3 (two clean: MOO1) · 2.5 (route-monism: HK; score+conquest+authored-crisis: Stellaris; two-martial-route: Gladius, HoMM3) · 2.0 (clock only: EU4; buyout-monism: OTC). **Paradox house signature: polity-maximal (4.5–5.0), endings-poor (2.0–2.5).**
- **P7 — Temporal form.** P7 = (discreteness + diegetic timescale)/2. Compression-collapse law: temporal compression atrophies the slow organs (see genealogy). Discreteness sub-scale: asynchronous/simultaneous turns 5 · active-pause ticks 2.5 (orders issuable while stopped — OTC, per rulebook §3.0) · suspension-pause continuous 0.5 (freeze only — discretizes attention, not action: SC2). Diegetic timescale: developmental years/eras 5 → operational minutes 1.

**Loop-spoke audit (derived field):** expansion↔economy · economy→ladder · ladder→capability · capability↔expansion.

## 1a. Chassis/House (v4.1 formal spec, standing)

- **Chassis (P1+P2+P3+P4+P6a+P7, /30) = the definition's operational clauses** — what the player does, with what, in what, as what, in what kind of time. *How the game runs.*
- **House (P5+P6b, /10) = the relational clauses** — with whom (peers) and for what (plural declared endings + institutional interiority). *Who the play is with, and what it is for.*
- Load-bearing test: full chassis + hollow house = functioning strategy game, not Civ-like (SC4). House without chassis degenerate by definition. Not a quality judgment; a change of basis. **Ch+H = Total asserted and EXECUTED per row (protocol 12, hardened).** Most extreme divergence pair: Dominions 6 (23.5/7.0) vs EU4 (21.5/8.5) — near-miss totals, opposite shapes.
- P6a = who drives (chassis); P6b = what the collective contains and where the journey ends (house). RV organ-map = demoted mnemonic (frame P1, drivetrain P2, engine P3, gearbox P4, neighborhood P5, driver's seat P6a, civic institutions P6b, transmission P7).

## 1b. Genealogy (v4.2 + v4.4 + v4.6 + v4.7 + v4.8 + v4.9)

**Ancestral duality (pre-1991):** the **solitaire builder** (chassis-rich, house-zero: SimCity) × the **wargame** (chassis-poor, **house-seeded**: the peer, the military polity-domain, the conquest teleology — a minimal house in our own terms). **Wargame axiom:** the wargame requires an opposing agent; script-agents do not confer peer-hood — the solitaire-wargame niche (Ambush!) faces scripts and converges on the tactics-game (XCOM, P5 0.5). No true solitary wargames at peer-grade.

**Civ's act (1991):** the builder's garden projected onto the wargame's polity skeleton, plus the arc (SimCity + Empire/Risk + the Civilization boardgame). Plural endings' parentage: conquest from the wargame; the mega-project/spaceship from the **builder** — completion-victory is the garden's teleology.

**Two fates of the synthesis:** (1) **house-elaboration** — the Civ line drifting toward society-simulation (culture Civ3, religion Civ4, the completed house); (2) the **Exterminate-weighted branch** — MOO's tactical battles, MoM following; the wing Emrich named "4X" at (1993). Founding trio below every mainline Civ — genealogically explained. **Paradox grand strategy = post-Civ offshoot** (no house-rich pre-Civ tradition existed to hybridize with; EU-lineage took the elaborated-house pole and shed the garden).

**"Civ-like" vs "4X":** two poles of one configuration. All Civ-likes perform the four X's (subset relation); 4X licenses heavy Exterminate-weighting. The community's "4X" label decomposes as **verb-completeness × long arc**: EU4 fails eXplore (P1 3.5) → grand strategy; OTC lacks eXterminate (WS 0, no units) → economic RTS; Anno peripheral-war + compressed → builder; Dominions/Stellaris pass both gates → 4X. Confirmed retrodiction: **4X label ↔ WS ≥ Substantial AND exploration present.**

**RTSes = selectively compressed 4X configurations (compression filter):** temporal compression does not shrink the configuration uniformly — it preserves the **fast organs** (resource streams, tier races, map contest, unit control) and atrophies the **slow organs** (gardens, epochal rewrites, polity, plural endings). Triple correspondence: Civ↔AoE, MOO↔SC, MoM↔WC3. **Compression-triple ordering confirmed (v4.7): AoE2 26.0 > SC2 24.0 > WC3 23.5; WC3 = SC2 − 0.5 = exactly the MoM hero-tax.**

**Wings:** fantasy — specialization 1994–2003 (MoM 34.5 → HoMM3 26.0 → Warlords 24.0), re-synthesis +1.0/generation 2003–2023 (SM 30.5 → AoW3 31.5 → AoW4 32.5). Test-rig siblings: OTC (24.5) ≈ CoE5 (25.0) — Civ4's and Dominions' engines in test rigs, from opposite design houses (OTC = "the economy alone is not the configuration"; CoE5 = citadel-anchored defeat purer than pretender-fusion). Garden-vs-time mirror: Anno (garden-first, P7 2.5) vs EU4 (polity-first, P7 4.0) — near-miss totals, mirrored shapes. **Proxy Studios (v4.8): the house-free house** — Gladius = the Exterminate branch taken to its pure end (full chassis, relational layer surgically removed, cities razed never captured); ZEPHON = the war wing walking back toward the center; Proxy signature: no-occupation war, 10-tier research, loyalty-stat, rebuildable instrument-heroes. ### War-Share (WS) — formalized v4.9 (from v4.2 §1c; Pure added v4.8; column + full re-audit this round)

**Definition:** position on the builder↔wargame axis of the ancestral duality — the wargame tradition's share of the design (military systems + conquest teleology; adversarialness alone is not war-share — OTC is all-adversarial, zero wargame). **Orthogonal to the pillar metrics:** judged, not computed; evidence lives in P5 (diplomacy), P6b (endings), and chassis telos, but WS is not a function of them (EU4: P5 5.0, WS 50 · Dominions: P5 3.5, WS 75 · OTC: adversarial house, WS 0). Unscored — never a pillar input. Levels are judged bands ("or close to" implicit); displayed as scale points in the table's WS column.

**Three probes:** (1) **Diplomacy** — in-game peer-diplomacy systems that materially suppress war (out-of-band human negotiation in MP never counts; scored form is SP default, protocol 15). (2) **Endings** — any declared victory route that is non-martial (armed force not the winning instrument). (3) **Telos** — chassis elements with non-martial telos (cultivation as its own end: wonders, culture, aesthetic flourishing) vs. everything reducing to war fuel.

**Levels (0/25/50/75/100):**
- **0 — Absent:** no armed-conflict layer at strategic scale. *SC4, Sims 4, FM26, OTC (sabotage/buyout, no units).*
- **25 — Peripheral:** conflict real but secondary; cultivation the center of gravity. *Anno (naval war real but secondary), HK, Col (economy the game, the independence war the exam — boundary case, mirror of OTC: martial endings, low war-share).*
- **50 — Substantial:** war a major verb among several within a full house. *The Civ line generally (Civ1–7, CtP pair, SMAC, BE, OW), EL, CK3, EU4 (v4.2 straddle resolved: war-central loop, but the house is full — Core requires vestigial residue), EVE (nullsec war one pillar among several), Stellaris, MOO pair, MoM, the GalCiv line.*
- **75 — Core:** war the dominant resolution layer; at least one probe shows builder-side residue. *Dominions 6 (binding NAPs — probe 1), the AoW line (diplomacy), Warlords (stances-grade), AoE2 (stances + tribute), TWH3, ZEPHON (standard-tier diplomacy, subordinate).*
- **100 — Pure:** all three probes clean — no in-game peer diplomacy AND exclusively martial victory routes AND every chassis element reduces to war fuel (user's criteria, v4.9). *Gladius, CoE5, HoMM3 (Grail = fossil; income function = war fuel), SC2, WC3. **Pure signature: P5 3.0 and H 5.0–5.5 across all five members — the level imposes a house ceiling; no Pure game can reach the space wing's 35.5 band even at full chassis.***
- **— N/A:** neither ancestral pole present as design telos. *Doom, ItB, XCOM 2 (reclassified from v4.2's Core anchor: per the wargame axiom, script-agents never conferred peer-hood — XCOM is the tactics-game, and its builder pole is war-fuel service).*

**Re-audit findings (v4.9):** the level↔score mapping is non-monotonic and overlapping — Absent 15.5–24.5 · Peripheral 32.0–37.5 · Substantial 26.5–38.5 · Core 24.0–32.5 · Pure 23.5–29.5. The v4.6–v4.7 "war-share clusters" (Substantial 35.5–38.0 vs Core 24.0–32.5) were **wing artifacts**: the space wing happens to combine Substantial war-share with full chassis. WS alone is not a score predictor; it is one axis of the registration declaration (protocol 2: level + evidence at lodging + wing-anchored bands). The distribution's middle bulge (23 of 46 at Substantial) is the genre's center of mass: the Civ synthesis's equilibrium.
