## 1. The Constitution (v3.4, unchanged)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite, world-scale map into a self-authored network of productive nodes whose cultivation is the game's center of gravity — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long, discretely-paced timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The eight pillars:**

- **P1 — Finite, hidden, world-scale map (the commons).** Clauses: initially unknown; finite; contested commons; diegetic spatial scale. Notes: generated maps price better than mirrored arenas on discovery (AoE2); resource-commons without territory prices below territorial commons; Dominions = the contiguous-world 5.0 standard (adjacency, chokepoints, thrones).
- **P2 — Generative expansion.** Planting, not seizing; authored network; developed nodes; minimal-foothold arc. **Acquisition tier, five members: EU4 (diplomatic), CK3 (dynastic), TWH3 (military), Dominions 6 (military-ritual), Anno-adjacent commercial — all 3.0. The ledger's most robust discriminator.**
- **P3 — Interior economy.** Center of gravity; self-improving nodes; evolving wide/deep dial. War-fuel inversion ×4 (SC2, TWH3, AoE2, partial-Dominions). Dominion scales noted: the deepest society-economy coupling scored (religion dynamically re-encodes province yields).
- **P4 — Epochal ladder.** (A) time as competition; (B) rewrites; (C) society re-encoding. **Ladder-shape taxonomy: frozen (Civ5) · commitment (BE) · shallow-swappable (EL) · frozen-with-exits (OW) · bestowed (Dominions — creation-time society propagating territorially) · swappable (full strength).** Match-scale epochs price on content, not duration (AoE2's four ages = 3.5 vs SC2's tier-increments = 3.0; P4 continuity verdict confirmed).
- **P5 (H) — Peer competitors.** Symmetric verbs; trajectories; diplomacy architecture; AI at equal-rules reference; no re-charging absences priced elsewhere. Dominions: the only total diplomacy void at ecosystem scale (famous by design).
- **P6a — The collective subject.** Leak-detector. Severity ladder: 5.0 pure · 4.5 single leak · 4.0 substitutive relay (Civ7) or **theocratic fusion (Dominions: person-as-subject −1.0, heroes −0.5, +0.5 god-persisting-through-dominion — the first mechanical person-collective fusion)** · 3.5 person-within-collective / person-as-subject · 3.0 person-primary + corporate credit · 2.0 persons-inverted · 1.0 micro-collective · 0 none.
- **P6b (H) — Total polity, plural endings.** P6b = (polity + endings)/2. Dominions (4.0+3.0): religion maximal and coequal; elimination + throne ascension. AoE2 (2.0+2.5): war-organs; conquest + wonder.
- **P7 — Temporal form.** P7 = (discreteness + diegetic timescale)/2. Compression-collapse law. Sub-scales: asynchronous/simultaneous turns 5 · active-pause ticks 2.5 · suspension-pause continuous 0.5. **AoE2's 1.5 = first in column (continuous play, centuries-spanning fiction — above SC2's timeless skirmish). Dominions 4.5 (simultaneous turns, months-per-turn years-long arcs).**

**Loop-spoke audit (derived field):** expansion↔economy · economy→ladder · ladder→capability · capability↔expansion. Worked examples on file.

**Chassis/house:** chassis = P1+P2+P3+P4+P6a+P7 (/30); house = P5+P6b (/10). Standing column; Ch+H = Total asserted per row. Inverse pairs to date: SC4/SC2, CK3/SC2-adjacent, TWH3/CK3, OTC/SC2, **Dominions/EU4 (23.5/6.5 vs 21.5/8.5 — the most extreme)**.
