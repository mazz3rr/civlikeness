## 1. The Constitution (v3.1, unchanged)

**Definition:** A Civ-like is a game in which the player, as a persistent collective identity, converts a minimal foothold on an initially unknown, finite, world-scale map into a self-authored network of productive nodes — in parallel competition with peers doing the same — across a long, discretely-paced timeline whose research ladder continually rewrites the rules, with several non-equivalent routes to a declared victory.

**The eight pillars:**

- **P1 — Finite, hidden, world-scale map (the commons).** Clauses: (1) initially unknown; (2) finite; (3) contested commons (encroachment, chokepoints, adjacency); (4) diegetic spatial scale (world+; deficits price, surpluses don't bonus). Values: 5.0 (×12) · 4.5 (MOO pair; Anno; **EVE** — strongest territorial commons in gaming, known-space cartography the one soft clause, WH-space the true frontier) · 3.5 (EU4/CK3 atlas; SC2) · 3.0 (**XCOM 2**: hidden world-scale geoscape, asymmetric contest) · 1.0 (Doom: finite + real layout-exploration, vestigial) · 0.5 (**ItB**: finite boards, perfect-information signature; **Sims 4**: lot boundaries) · **0 (FM26: the league table is not territory — first P1 zero; old boundary clause 3 absorbed as a booked zero)**.
- **P2 — Generative expansion.** Planting, not seizing; authored network; developed nodes. Values: 5.0 (×13) · 4.0 (SC2, SC4) · 3.5 (**EVE**: construction + conquest hybrid) · 3.0 (EU4, CK3, **Sims 4**: generation without expansion — one garden that never sprawls) · 2.5 (**XCOM 2**: one authored node (Avenger) + contact network) · 1.5 (**FM26**: one inherited node, genuinely developed) · **0 (Doom, ItB — P2's first zeros; gate-floor demolished)**.
- **P3 — Interior economy.** Center of gravity = developing what one owns; solitaire optimizer; evolving wide/deep dial. Values: 5.0 (×7) · 4.0 (**EVE**: deepest economy ever made; center shared with war/politics) · 3.5 (**Sims 4**: domestic economy, total center-of-gravity at micro-scale) · 3.0 (EU4, **FM26**: real club economy) · 2.5 (CK3, SC2, **XCOM 2**: instrumental, feeds the tactical object) · 0.5 (**Doom**: ammunition conservation as trace).
- **P4 — Epochal ladder.** (A) time as competition; (B) vocabulary rewrites; (C) society re-encoding. Branch density inadmissible. Values: 5.0 (×6) · 4.5 (×3) · 3.5 (×3) · 3.0 (EU4, CK3, SC2, **XCOM 2** — tree + doom-race + tool-rewrites, no society; **FM26** — tier transitions rewrite the game, season races, no research system) · 2.5 (Col, **Sims 4** — climbable unraced ladders, **EVE** — ship-class rewrites, no ladder) · 0.5 (**Doom**: escalation curve, not a system).
- **P5 — Peer competitors.** Symmetric verbs, trajectories, diplomacy, competence at equal-rules reference. Values: 5.0 (MOO pair, Civ4, EU4, **EVE** — first human-earned; the fifth member) · 4.5 (×7, incl. **FM26** — best rival simulation in the battery; AI counters across seasons) · 4.0 (×3) · 3.0 (SC2) · 0.5 (**XCOM 2**: Chosen as trace adversarial ecology) · 0 (SC4, **Doom, ItB, Sims 4**).
- **P6a — The collective subject.** Leak-detector: default-max, moves only on person-agency. Values: 5.0 (×11) · 4.5 (×3) · 4.0 (Civ7) · 3.5 (MoM, **EVE** — capsuleer person-within-collective) · 3.0 (CK3 — old in-domain floor, now mid-column) · 2.0 (**Sims 4** persons-as-whole-game + dynasty persistence; **XCOM 2** soldier-heroes at max leak over thin org; **FM26** manager-as-person, club-as-employer) · 1.0 (**ItB**: squad micro-collective, named leveling pilots) · **0 (Doom — the pillar's true zero; old boundary clause 2 absorbed as a booked zero)**.
- **P6b — Total polity, plural endings.** P6b = (polity + endings)/2. Values: 5.0 (×4) · 4.5 (×2) · 4.0 (×2) · 3.5 (×6) · 3.0 (MOO1) · 2.5 (Anno, HK, Stellaris) · 2.0 (SC2, EU4, CK3, **XCOM 2** [1.5+2.0→1.75→2.0, disclosed], **FM26** [2.0+1.5→1.75→2.0 — teleologies without termination: plural emergent declared trophies that never end the career]) · 1.5 (**EVE** [3.0+0]) · 1.0 (SC4, **Sims 4** [1.0+0.5→0.75→1.0]) · 0.5 (**Doom, ItB** [0+0.5→0.25→0.5: authored narrative endpoints]). Column continuous 0.5→5.0.
- **P7 — Temporal form.** P7 = (discreteness + diegetic timescale)/2. Compression-collapse law. Values: 5.0 (×12) · 4.0 (EU4, CK3, Stellaris, **XCOM 2** [5+2.5→3.75→4.0 — campaign is developmental, not operational; registration miss disclosed]) · 3.5 (**FM26** — the Continue button is a turn; player fully discretizes; seasons-to-decades) · 3.0 (Anno, **ItB** [5+1], **Sims 4** [3+3: pause-able, life-scale]) · 2.0 (SC4, **EVE** [0.5+3: no pause, uncompressed real-time — the anti-SC2]) · 1.0 (SC2) · 0.5 (**Doom** — continuous, operational, no internal temporal structure).
